# Meaning registry sources

`concepts.csv` is built by `build_concepts.py` from **Concepticon** (concepticon-data repository, commit 795faa6, November 2026; data of release 3.4.0): List, Johann Mattis & Tjuka, Annika & Blum, Frederic & Kučerová, Alžběta & Ugarte, Carlos Barrientos & Rzymski, Christoph & Greenhill, Simon J. & Forkel, Robert (eds.) 2025. *Concepticon v3.4.0. A Resource for the Linking of Concept Lists.* Leipzig: Max Planck Institute for Evolutionary Anthropology. https://concepticon.clld.org. Licensed CC BY 4.0.

The tiers come from Concepticon's links to these concept lists:

- Swadesh's lists (1950–1971) and the Leipzig–Jakarta list (Tadmor 2009): tier 1, core vocabulary
- The World Loanword Database (Haspelmath & Tadmor 2009) and the Intercontinental Dictionary Series (Key & Comrie 2016): tier 2, basic vocabulary

The rule that colour terms enter in the Berlin and Kay (1969) order is the project's own, as is everything in `templates.json`.

English glosses are labels for meanings, not the source of any word. No UTP language's vocabulary is derived from English.
