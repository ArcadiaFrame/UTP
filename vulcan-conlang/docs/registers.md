# The body, sex, insults and slang: registers by era

Every language layers its intimate and rough words, and the layers record its history. English keeps Old English four-letter words beside Latinate medical terms. Japanese keeps native words beside Chinese-derived ones and builds euphemisms on top. Vulcan's layers follow its own history: frank before Surak, indirect under Surak, clinical in the Federation, and slangy among the young. Every word here is in the dictionary with its register label: *vulgar*, *euphemism*, *clinical*, *colloquial* or *slang*.

## The layers

| Era | What happened | Register today | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tribal and Classical | An emotive, feudal society named the body and sex plainly | **vulgar**: the old frank roots survive only as coarse words | *keknil*, *tineeth*, *mitan*, *shath* "have sex" |
| Unified (Surak) | Speaking of the body became speaking of passion, so the frank words were set aside for indirect ones, as *ozh'esta* gave way to *sileil* | **euphemism** | *ur-lu* "the self below," *var'e-kau* "joined skin," *katra-sheleuk* "carrying a katra," *plurpuhl* "the necessary place" (the Latin *necessarium* made the same move) |
| Standard (Federation medicine) | Neutral compounds for healers and formal speech | **clinical** | *mik t'si'an* anatomy ("inquiry of form"), *kanuh-khasni* "male part," *skon-khasni* "female part," *unak-tulek* "seed-vessel," *hafei-plak* "month-blood" |
| Standard (23rd–24th century, the young) | Clipping, doubling, the diminutive *-kam*, and Human loans | **colloquial**, **slang** | *chekh-kam* buddy, *wan-kam* kid, *sa-sa* nope, *ni-ni* really, *kthi* "logical" as approval, *okei* okay |
| Romulan | The frank words never fell out of use, and intimacy follows rank | everyday and colloquial | *aefvei* consort (of a superior), *llaenn* favorite, *mnaerh* flirt, *fvaesh* damn |

A Standard Vulcan in polite company uses the clinical or the euphemistic word. The old coarse root on a Vulcan's lips means lost control, a Classical-era speaker, or a deliberate insult.

## Gender

- **Pronouns are gender-neutral.** *ish-veh* is "that one," for anyone. Gender shows in names (canon *T'* for women, *S* for men) and in kin words, and in Romulan in the house prefixes *t'* and *tr'*.
- **Words:**
  - male *sutshish*, female *sashir*, gender *si'an-uhak* ("form-kind")
  - masculine *kanuhuk*, feminine *skonuk*
  - boy *kanuh-kam* and girl *skon-kam* (with canon *-kam*, "little")
  - androgynous *chau-si'anuk*
- **Standard Vulcan, shaped by IDIC, adds:**
  - *ko-si'anuk*, of another form than man or woman
  - *katra-si'an-veh*, a transgender person: one whose bodily form is brought to match the katra. The katra is the self, and the form follows it.

## Sexuality

Classical Vulcan **describes attraction rather than naming identities**, as most pre-modern societies did:

- *kanuh-seekauvik*, drawn to men · *skon-seekauvik*, drawn to women · *chau-seekauvik*, drawn to both
- **Bonds:** *keit'k'ul-koon*, a bond between two of one form, and *ko-koon*, a bond between two of different form. A bond is a bond, and *t'hy'la* asks nothing about form at all.
- **The rest:**
  - mating *lulur*, intercourse *luluruth* (clinical), consummate a bond *koon-kari*
  - arousal *peltakh-karuth* ("kindled heat," the fire metaphor of pon farr), lust *moom-peltakh*, attraction *kahol* (from the novels' *kah*, "the pull of pon farr")
  - lover *var'e-mati*, infidelity *koon-sarheuth*, flirting *hirlek-nol* ("eye-play")
  - chaste *uhf-luluruk*, chastity *uhf-lulurukhol* (the Gol ascetics), virgin *uhf-lulur-veh*
  - naked *uhf-talmil*, modesty *k'ulhol*

## Insults and oaths

| Word | Meaning | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| *uhf-mok* | houseless, of no lineage | the gravest, in a society of Houses |
| *k'ikal-plak* | half-breed | offensive; the bullies' word in *Star Trek* (2009) |
| *neri-veh* | coward | grave |
| *nirak* | fool (canon) | sharp |
| *suthnoo-veh* | "displayer," one who shows emotion | a schoolyard taunt |
| *suhkthi* | illogical! | the everyday Vulcan rebuke |
| *sehlat-shaffal* | sehlat dung! | coarse curse |
| *far eh mazhiv* | fire and sand! | old oath, now coarse |
| *ponfo miran* | go to hell (canon) | coarse |
| *haerrin* (Romulan) | "one who stayed behind": a Vulcan | Romulan contempt |

## How slang is made

| Process | Example |
|---|---|
| Clipping | *suhkthi* from *suhk-kthiauk*; Romulan *khre'shi* "boss" from *khre'riov* |
| Doubling | *sa-sa* "nope," *ni-ni* "really" |
| Diminutive *-kam* | *chekh-kam* "little friend," *wan-kam* "little child" |
| Meaning shift | *kthi* "logical" as approval, the way English uses "cool" |
| Loans from Federation English | *okei* okay |

The Phrasebook's scene "cadet slang" shows these in use, and shows an elder answering slang with the Standard form.
