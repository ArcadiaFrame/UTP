# The Vulcan language timeline

Each dictionary entry has an era. The eras sit on the Vulcan cultural timeline, so a writer can tell which words a character of any period would know. Dates before the Time of Awakening are design; the rest follow canon.

| Era | When | Society | Language | Sound | How words are made |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Tribal** | Before c. 6000 BCE (design) | Desert clans; raw touch telepathy; the bond and the challenge (*koon-ut*, *kal-if-fee*) | Proto-Vulcan, one language spread thin across many clan dialects | Harsh: ejectives, *q*, *lh*, nasal clusters | Roots; compounds by juxtaposition (*plak tow*, *pon far*); *-tor*, *ve*, *kau*, *-ik* |
| **Classical** | c. 6000 BCE to the 4th century CE | Feudal Houses (Edo Japan and the Roman Republic and Empire); the orders before Surak; mind-training begins about 3,000 years before Surak (VOY "Random Thoughts") | Ceremonial Vulcan (the Gol liturgy) and regional languages | Still harsh; *ai* > *ei*, nasal clusters voice (*nt* > *d*) | The building endings become productive: *-uk*, *-hol*, *-uth*, *-puhl*; honorific *-mush* and humble *-fum* |
| **Unified** | The Time of Awakening, 4th century CE, into the 21st century | Surak's reform during atomic war; the Sundering (the Romulans leave); planetary unity; industry | Unified Vulcan, one planetary standard; Romulan splits away at the Sundering | Smoothing begins | Surakian coinages: the noting particle *kon*, the formal particle *suh*, *kolinahr*, IDIC (*sarohol uhf-nawakuk spushuth uhf-nawakuk*) |
| **Standard** | First contact (2063) through the 24th century; Federation member from 2161 | The High Command, the Syrrannite restoration, then the Federation | Federation Standard Vulcan | Smooth by the 2240s: *q* > *k*, *lh* > *l* | Technical compounds (*ur-zhoko*, *zhoko-shelekpuhl*, *torrir-koshasowash*); loans (*komihn* "Human") |
| **Romulan** | The Sundering (4th century) to the 24th century | The Rihannsu Empire; Houses, Senate, Tal Shiar | Romulan, an independent daughter language | Ejectives become *kh*, *th*, *fv*; *s-* becomes *r-*; vowels break into *ae* and *oa* | Its own coinages and mode endings (*-'nra* upward, *-ha* downward) |

## Using the eras in fiction

- **A 23rd-century Vulcan** speaks Standard, and recites Ceremonial forms in rites (*pon far*, the Gol liturgy).
- **Old Vulcan words** (register "archaic") are fossils, quoted rather than spoken: *qualsetu*, *zchliak*.
- **A Romulan** shares only the pre-Sundering words with a Vulcan, and has reshaped even those: Vulcan *sahrafel* is Romulan *rarafael*.
- **A word dated Standard** would not be heard in a pre-Surak scene.
