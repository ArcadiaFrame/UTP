# The body in Vulcan: anatomy, physiology and clinical words

This guide covers the words for the body, its systems, and Vulcan clinical medicine, including the cycle vocabulary of pon farr. It applies the setting's species design notes (18 November 2026) to the dictionary: every word here sits in the dictionary with its era, register and parts. Batch 24 added most of them.

**How the words are made.** A concept gets a new root only when every culture names it with a basic word (body, fat, guts, kidney, milk, navel, sinew, gland). Everything else is built from older words, the way Latin, Japanese and German build their medical vocabularies. Clinical words date from the Standard era and sound like it: long and transparent, like German *Herzklappe* "heart-valve".

## 10. A clinical passage

Each line is in SOV order, with the evidential last. The evidential tells the reader how each claim is known: *{ev-seen}* means measured or observed, *{ev-reported}* means the patient said so, *{ev-inferred}* means the healer concluded it.

1. *{healer} {patient-n}-{object-wizh} {examine}-{past-un} {ev-seen}.*
   healer patient-OBJ examine-PAST seen. "The healer examined the patient."
2. *{core-temperature} {low} {ev-seen}.*
   core-temperature low seen. "Core temperature is low." (measured)
3. *{patient-n} {bond-ache}-{object-wizh} {feel} {ev-reported}.*
   patient bond-ache-OBJ feel reported. "The patient reports bond-ache."
4. *{long-cycle} {fever-threshold}-{object-wizh} {not} {perfect-ki}{pass-through} {ev-inferred}.*
   long-cycle fever-threshold-OBJ not has-passed inferred. "The cycle has not passed the fever threshold, I conclude."
5. *{if} {fever-spiral} {start}, {healer} {healer-link}-{object-wizh} {must} {start} {ev-inferred}.*
   if fever-spiral begin, healer healer-link-OBJ must begin inferred. "If the fever spiral begins, a healer must begin a healer's link."
6. *{without} {informed-consent}, {healer} {healer-link}-{object-wizh} {not} {may} {start}.*
   without informed-consent, healer healer-link-OBJ not may begin. "Without informed consent, a healer may not begin the link."
7. *{conception} {outside} {pon far} {may} {happen} {ev-reported}.*
   conception outside pon-farr may happen reported. "Conception can happen outside pon farr, it is reported."
