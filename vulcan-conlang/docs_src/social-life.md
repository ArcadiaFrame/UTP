# Social life, survival, bonds and rank

Batch 21 adds the vocabulary of living among others in a hard land: how Vulcans receive a guest and keep a reputation, how they confide and court, how a desert people stays alive, and how the war-Houses and the training halls rank their members. Batch 22 adds life inside a bond: what the bond feels, comfort, quarrels and their mending, and parting. Most words are built from older roots, because these are social and institutional meanings (see `word-building.md`). Every form below renders from the dictionary.

## The heart-whisper

*{confide-heart}* (verb) and *{heart-whisper}* (noun), "heart-whisper," name one act: confiding a matter of the heart about someone who is not present. It covers telling a t'hy'la "I am drawn to T'Lara," telling a friend "she is drawn to you," and weighing a possible courtship with family before anyone approaches.

- **Who may hear it.** Only bond-kin: t'hy'la, bondmates, siblings. The same whispered sentence told to anyone else is *{rumor}*, rumor ("a whispering"). The two words share a root on purpose: the difference is the listener.
- **The one who keeps it** is the *{confidant}*, "heart-keeper." To keep a confidence is *{keep-confidence}*.
- **The declaration** made to the person desired is a different act: *{declare-feeling}*, "heart-tell."
- **Standard grammar keeps its discipline even here.** The absent one's feeling is never stated bare: *{attraction} {in} {ish-ve} {ev-inferred}*, "there is attraction in her, I conclude." A Standard Vulcan in a heart-whisper still says how they know.

## Life in the bond

Batch 22 adds the vocabulary of life between bondmates and t'hy'la. Two facts shape it. Vulcans are touch-telepaths, so closeness is described as what the mind and the bond feel. And Standard reserve still holds in private, so most of these words let a feeling be reported as evidence rather than displayed.

**What the bond reports.** These three words are statements of fact, so they take *{ev-melded}*, "known through touch or the bond":

| Vulcan | English |
|---|---|
| *{bond-warmth}* | bond-warmth: the other's presence felt through the bond |
| *{bond-ache}* | bond-ache: the pain of a long distance |
| *{bond-silence}* | bond-silence: the stillness where a bonded mind is no longer felt, through great distance or death |

A Standard Vulcan who would never say "I missed you" can say *{bond-silence} {long}*, "the bond-silence was long," and has said it. The frank word exists too: *{miss}*, "miss" ("feel the distance"), with its noun *{missing}*. Said in Standard, it is still named, not displayed: *{missing} {in} {nash-ve} {noting}*.
