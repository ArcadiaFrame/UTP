# The body, sex, insults and slang: registers by era

Every language layers its intimate and rough words, and the layers record its history. English keeps Old English four-letter words beside Latinate medical terms. Japanese keeps native words beside Chinese-derived ones and builds euphemisms on top. Vulcan's layers follow its own history: frank before Surak, indirect under Surak, clinical in the Federation, and slangy among the young. Every word here is in the dictionary with its register label: *vulgar*, *euphemism*, *clinical*, *colloquial* or *slang*.

## The layers

| Era | What happened | Register today | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tribal and Classical | An emotive, feudal society named the body and sex plainly | **vulgar**: the old frank roots survive only as coarse words | *{penis-v}*, *{vagina-v}*, *{arse}*, *{mate-v}* "have sex" |
| Unified (Surak) | Speaking of the body became speaking of passion, so the frank words were set aside for indirect ones, as *{ozh'esta}* gave way to *{finger-touch}* | **euphemism** | *{genitals-e}* "the self below," *{intercourse-e}* "joined skin," *{pregnant-e}* "carrying a katra," *{toilet-e}* "the necessary place" (the Latin *necessarium* made the same move) |
| Standard (Federation medicine) | Neutral compounds for healers and formal speech | **clinical** | *{anatomy}* anatomy ("inquiry of form"), *{penis-c}* "male part," *{vagina-c}* "female part," *{testicles-c}* "seed-vessel," *{menstruation}* "month-blood" |
| Standard (23rd–24th century, the young) | Clipping, doubling, the diminutive *-kam*, and Human loans | **colloquial**, **slang** | *{buddy}* buddy, *{kid}* kid, *{nope}* nope, *{really}* really, *{logical-s}* "logical" as approval, *{okay-loan}* okay |
| Romulan | The frank words never fell out of use, and intimacy follows rank | everyday and colloquial | *aefvei* consort (of a superior), *llaenn* favorite, *mnaerh* flirt, *fvaesh* damn |

A Standard Vulcan in polite company uses the clinical or the euphemistic word. The old coarse root on a Vulcan's lips means lost control, a Classical-era speaker, or a deliberate insult.

## Gender

- **Pronouns are gender-neutral.** *{ish-ve}* is "that one," for anyone. Gender shows in names (canon *T'* for women, *S* for men) and in kin words, and in Romulan in the house prefixes *t'* and *tr'*.
- **Words:**
  - male *{male}*, female *{female}*, gender *{gender}* ("form-kind")
  - masculine *{masculine}*, feminine *{feminine}*
  - boy *{boy}* and girl *{girl}* (with canon *-kam*, "little")
  - androgynous *{both-form}*
- **Standard Vulcan, shaped by IDIC, adds:**
  - *{other-form}*, of another form than man or woman
  - *{katra-form}*, a transgender person: one whose bodily form is brought to match the katra. The katra is the self, and the form follows it.

## Sexuality

Classical Vulcan **describes attraction rather than naming identities**, as most pre-modern societies did:

- *{drawn-men}*, drawn to men · *{drawn-women}*, drawn to women · *{drawn-both}*, drawn to both
- **Bonds:** *{same-bond}*, a bond between two of one form, and *{other-bond}*, a bond between two of different form. A bond is a bond, and *{t'hy'la}* asks nothing about form at all.
- **The rest:**
  - mating *{mating}*, intercourse *{intercourse-c}* (clinical), consummate a bond *{consummate}*
  - arousal *{arousal}* ("kindled heat," the fire metaphor of pon farr), lust *{lust}*, attraction *{attraction}* (from the novels' *kah*, "the pull of pon farr")
  - lover *{lover-b}*, infidelity *{infidelity}*, flirting *{flirt}* ("eye-play")
  - chaste *{chaste}*, chastity *{chastity}* (the Gol ascetics), virgin *{virgin}*
  - naked *{naked}*, modesty *{modesty}*

## Insults and oaths

| Word | Meaning | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| *{houseless}* | houseless, of no lineage | the gravest, in a society of Houses |
| *{half-breed}* | half-breed | offensive; the bullies' word in *Star Trek* (2009) |
| *{coward}* | coward | grave |
| *{nirak}* | fool (canon) | sharp |
| *{show-off}* | "displayer," one who shows emotion | a schoolyard taunt |
| *{illogical-s}* | illogical! | the everyday Vulcan rebuke |
| *{sehlat-dung}* | sehlat dung! | coarse curse |
| *{fire-and-sand}* | fire and sand! | old oath, now coarse |
| *ponfo miran* | go to hell (canon) | coarse |
| *haerrin* (Romulan) | "one who stayed behind": a Vulcan | Romulan contempt |

## How slang is made

| Process | Example |
|---|---|
| Clipping | *{illogical-s}* from *{illogical}*; Romulan *khre'shi* "boss" from *khre'riov* |
| Doubling | *{nope}* "nope," *{really}* "really" |
| Diminutive *-kam* | *{buddy}* "little friend," *{kid}* "little child" |
| Meaning shift | *{logical-s}* "logical" as approval, the way English uses "cool" |
| Loans from Federation English | *{okay-loan}* okay |

The Phrasebook's scene "cadet slang" shows these in use, and shows an elder answering slang with the Standard form.
