# Vulcan grammar reference

Federation Standard Vulcan, with notes on the older layers and on Romulan. Every example renders from the dictionary. The Design Bible's Grammar tab holds the full reasoning behind each rule, and `docs/word-building.md` covers how words are made.

## 1. Word order

**Subject, object, verb, with the evidential last.** Canon *Pod Tucker avalde keru* shows the noun-first noun phrase; *Dungi tu sarafel?* shows the verb at the end.

*{ship} {here}{toward} {come}-{past-un} {ev-seen}.* "The ship came here. (I saw it.)"

- **Noun phrases are head first:** noun, then adjective, then number, then possessor. *{house} {old} {my}*, "my old house" · *{cousin}-{plural} {twelve}*, "twelve cousins."
- **"Of" phrases** take *t'*: *{inquiry} t'{star}*, "inquiry of stars" (astronomy).
- **Prepositions** come before their noun: *{in} {house}*, "in the house" · *{from} {ship}*, "from the ship." Direction toward is the ending *-{toward}*: *{house}{toward}*, "to the house."
- **Subjects and objects drop** when context makes them clear. Canon *ki'sarlah*, "it has come," has no subject.

## 2. Nouns

| Ending | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| *-{plural}* | plural | *{ship}-{plural}*, ships |
| *-{object-wizh}* | object, on the last word of the object phrase | *{house} {my}-{object-wizh}*, my house (as object) |
| *-{toward}* | toward, to | *{house}{toward}*, to the house |
| *-{kam}* | dear, little (canon) | *Saavik-kam*, little Saavik |

In casual speech, *-{object-wizh}* may drop for an indefinite thing (*{question} {tu} {tea} {want}?*, "Would you like tea?"). It stays for people and for anything definite.

## 4. Verbs

| Form | How | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Present | bare verb | *{nash-ve} {water} {drink}.* "I drink water." |
| Ongoing | *-{ongoing}* | *{nash-ve} {work}-{ongoing}.* "I am working." |
| Past | *-{past-un}* (after *-{ongoing}*) | *{work}-{ongoing}-{past-un}*, "was working" |
| Completed | *{perfect-ki}-* | *{perfect-ki}{come}*, "has come" (canon *ki'sarlah*) |
| Future | *{dungi}* before the verb | *{dungi} {tu} {trust}?* "Will you trust (me)?" (canon) |
| Ability, duty, permission, advice | *{kup}*, *{must}*, *{may}*, *{should}* before the verb | *{nash-ve} {kup} {help}.* "I can help." |
| Hypothetical | *{would}* | "would" |
| Command | bare verb, subject dropped; *{request}* in front makes it a request | *{request} {nash-ve}-{object-wizh} {follow}.* "Please follow me." |
| "Let us" | *{hortative}* before the verb | *{hortative} {go}.* "Let us go." |
| Negation | *{not}* right before the verb; *{worla}* "never" takes the same slot; one negative per clause | *{nash-ve} {not} {understand}.* "I do not understand." |
| Causative | *-{causative}* | *{frighten}*, frighten |

**"To be."** Descriptions have no verb: *{house} {old}.* "The house is old." Identity uses *{be}*: canon *Saavik wimish*, "I am Saavik."

## 5. Questions and answers

- **Question words come first:** *{what}*, *{who}*, *{where}*, *{when}*, *{how}*, *{gishun}* why, *{which}*, *{whose}*, *{how-many}*. *{where} {tu} {go}-{ongoing}?* "Where are you going?"
- **Yes-or-no:** move the auxiliary to the front (*{kup} {tu} {help}?*), or open with *{question}*.
- **Answers:** *{yes}* yes and *{no}* no. More Vulcan is to repeat the verb (*{understand}.* "Understand.") or answer with an evidential alone (*{ev-seen}.* "I saw it.").

## 6. Evidentials: how you know

| Particle | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *{ev-seen}* | I observed it directly |
| *{ev-inferred}* | I conclude it by logic |
| *{ev-reported}* | I was told it |
| *{ev-melded}* | I know it through a meld or touch |

In Standard Vulcan, another person's emotion cannot be stated bare. *{ish-ve} {anger} {ev-inferred}.* "He is angry (I conclude)."

## 7. Speech levels

- **Honorific** *-{honorific}* raises the person spoken of or to. **Humble** *-{humble}* lowers the speaker.
- **Address:** *{vocative}* before a title or name, *{you-honored}* "you (honored)," and the sentence-final formal particle *{formal}*.
- **Honorific and humble verbs** replace the plain ones (see the dictionary's honorific and humble registers).
- **Between t'hy'la, every honorific drops.** Plain speech is itself the sign of the bond.

## 8. Feeling and touch

- **Classical (before Surak):** the one who feels is the subject. *{exclamative} {nash-ve} {rage}!* "How I rage!"
- **Standard:** the emotion is the subject, the speaker a place, and the noting particle closes it. *{anger} {in} {nash-ve} {noting}.* "There is anger in me. Noted."
- **After losing control:** *{request} {forgive-display}.* "Please forgive the display."
- **Touch requires leave:** *{request} {nash-ve} {touch-hum}?* "May this one touch you?" The reply is *{grant-leave}* (granted) or *{mind-shield}* (refused).

## 9. Joining clauses

- **Joining words:** *{e}* and, *{but}* but, *{or}* or, *{therefore}* therefore, *{then}* then.
- **Clauses that come first:** *{if}* if, *{while}* while, *{until}* until, *{although}* although, *{when}* when. *{if} {tu} {come}, {we} {kal-toh} {dungi} {play}.* "If you come, we will play kal-toh."
- **Clauses that may follow:** *{because}* because, *{so-that}* so that.
- **Reported speech:** *{comp}* opens what was said or known, and the verb of saying comes last. *{ish-ve} {comp} {come}-{past-un} {say}-{past-un}.* "He said that he had come."
- **Relative clauses:** *{relative}* follows the noun. *{person} {relative} {ship}-{object-wizh} {repair}-{past-un}*, "the person who repaired the ship."

## 10. Comparison and manner

- **Comparison:** X, Y *{than}*, then *{more}* or *{less}*, then the quality. *{nash} {ish} {than} {more} {logical}.* "This is more logical than that."
- **Most:** *{most}* + quality: *{most} {big}*, largest.
- **Manner:** *-{adverbial}*: *{slow}{adverbial}*, slowly.

## 11. Romulan in brief

Romulan inherits this grammar and reshapes it. Mode endings mark rank on every predicate: *-'nra* is said up to a superior, *-ha* down to an inferior. The copula *aihr-* "this is" appears, and nouns take *-ha* singular and *-nsu* plural. There are no evidentials; instead *hwil* marks "not to be repeated" and *khnai* "it is whispered." See `docs/dictionary-romulan.md` and the bible's Romulan tab.
