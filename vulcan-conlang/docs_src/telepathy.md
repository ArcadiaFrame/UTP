# The mind sciences: Vulcan touch telepathy

Vulcan mind-training is older than Surak by some three thousand years. Much of what the old orders knew survived only as words in the liturgy. This document sets out the vocabulary as a working system.

What is canon:
- Touch strengthens telepathy, and the fingertips go to points on the face.
- Melds can share, probe, heal, harm and carry a katra.
- An untrained meld can cause pa'nar.
- The bioelectric focal points (*{qui'lari}*) exist.

What is design: the three channels of contact, the seven grades, and the lost words. All of it is built to fit the canon.

## How contact works: three channels

| Channel | Old name (Classical) | Standard name | What it carries |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Field** (*{field-channel}*) | *{life-heat}*, "life-heat," the warmth felt around a living person | *{bio-field}*, the bioelectric life-field, focused at the *{qui'lari}* | presence, intent, the sense that a mind is there |
| **Chemical** (*{salt-channel}*) | *{mind-salts}*, "mind-salts" | *{neurotransmitter}*, neurotransmitters; *{touch-exchange}*, the exchange skin to skin | emotional tone: calm, pain, fear |
| **Wave** (*{wave-channel}*) | *{mind-wave}*, the mind's rhythm | *{attunement}*, attunement: bringing two rhythms into one beat; the verb is *{attune}* | thought, image, memory |

The channels can work singly, together, or in sequence. The Classical healers knew them by feel: life-heat, mind-salts, rhythm. Standard science gave them clinical names. Mastery means learning to use each channel alone, then in order, then all at once. That is why touch matters so much: skin contact is the only way to open all three channels together. Contact begins at the meld points (*{meld-point}*), with the fingertips placed on them (*{meld-point-touch}*).

## Directions

- **Receptive** (*{receptive}*): taking in another mind, *{mind-receive}*.
- **Projective** (*{projective}*): sending one's own mind outward, *{mind-send}*, including the mind's voice (*{mind-voice}*), speech without words.
- **Reciprocal:** the full meld (*{meld}*, sacred *{commune}*), "minds joined," canon *kashkau*.
- **Group:** the bridging of minds (*{bridging}*), one melder linking several into a joined mind of many (*{group-mind}*).

## Scopes of reflection

| Scope | Vulcan | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Internal | *{inner-reflection}* | the mind turned on itself; at its deepest, the self-meld (*{self-meld}*), meditation into one's own katra |
| External | *{outer-reflection}* | reading another mind |
| Group | *{group-reflection}* | minds looking together |

## The seven grades

The Order of Communion ranked melders in seven grades. The grades passed into Surak's teaching and are kept at Mount Seleya. A master of the seventh grade is a *{master-melder}*.

| Grade | Name | Mastered | Channels |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | *{grade-open-hand}*, the Open Hand | sensing presence and mood through touch | field |
| 2 | *{grade-still-water}*, the Still Water | receiving surface thought and feeling | field, chemical |
| 3 | *{grade-sending}*, the Sending | projecting calm and simple thought; blocking pain | field, chemical, wave (one at a time) |
| 4 | *{grade-joining}*, the Joining | the full meld | all three at once |
| 5 | *{grade-deep}*, the Deep | memory, healing (*{fal-tor-vo}*), the edge of the katra | all three, deep |
| 6 | *{grade-bridge}*, the Bridge | linking several minds; contact at a distance | all three, many minds |
| 7 | *{grade-carrying}*, the Carrying | holding and passing a katra (*{katra-give}*, *{vre'katra}*, *{fal-tor-pan}*) | all three, beyond the body |

An eighth grade, *{lost-eighthgrade}*, is named in the old texts, but its practice is lost.

## Bonds

- **Forming a bond:** *{bond-form}* forms a mental bond, as in the canon childhood bonding.
- **Distance:** *{bond-sense}* is feeling a bondmate across distance.
- **Ending one:** *{bond-sever}* severs a bond.
- **What remains:** after any meld, each mind keeps a residue of the other, *{meld-ash}*, "meld-ash." Canon notes that part of a katra can remain.

## Dangers and crimes

- *{bleed-over}*, bleed-over: another's feeling floods one's own. This is the risk of an untrained meld, and of an untrained melder (*{untrained-melder}*), as with Tolaris.
- *{pa'nar}*: the disease a bad meld can cause (canon).
- *{forced-meld}* forced meld, *{mind-theft}* theft from a mind, *{seize-mind}* seizing a mind, *{mind-break}* breaking one. These are the gravest crimes.
- Erasing a memory or planting a suggestion without leave is a violation, whatever the motive.
- **Consent comes first.** *{request} {nash-ve} {touch-hum}?* "May this one touch you?" The answer is *{grant-leave}*, or a refusal with *{mind-shield}*.

## What was lost

These words survived in the Gol liturgy and the Mount Seleya rites, but the practices behind them did not. Their meanings are reconstructed, and they make good story hooks.

| Word | What is guessed |
|---|---|
| *{lost-bloodread}* | an old hand-art of the Communion; perhaps "reading the blood" |
| *{lost-stonehear}* | the "third hearing": perhaps sensing a mind through stone or ground |
| *{lost-firsttouch}* | the moment two minds first meet; perhaps "the spark" |
| *{lost-ravenmind}* | a forbidden war-mind art of the Order of the Raptor; only the name survives |
| *{lost-katraword}* | a liturgical word of the katra rites, untranslated even in Surak's time |
| *{lost-eighthgrade}* | a grade beyond the seventh |

## By era

| Era | Practice |
|---|---|
| Tribal | Raw touch telepathy, used in the bond, the challenge and the hunt; the words are roots (*{meld}*, *{touch}*, *{katra}*) |
| Classical | The orders: the Communion ranks its grades, the Raptor turns minds into weapons, and the folk names arise (life-heat, mind-salts) |
| Unified | Surak codifies consent and keeps the grades; the channel names are formalized |
| Standard | Science names the mechanisms (bio-field, neurotransmitter). The 22nd-century High Command shuns melders (*{melder}* becomes a slur) until the Syrrannites restore the practice |
| Romulan (design) | The Romulans kept touch as an instrument of rank. Their old melding words survive mainly in the language of interrogation |
