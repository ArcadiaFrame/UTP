# Andorian language and culture on screen (canon)

Compiled for the UTP from Memory Alpha (memory-alpha.fandom.com: "Andorian language", "Aenar", "Weytahn", "Shran") and episode transcripts, November 2026. Summaries in the compiler's words; episode references in brackets.

## The language itself

Andorian is the language Andorians speak on Andoria. Hoshi Sato noticed that the frequency distortions of an alien symbiont looked like the phonetic patterns of certain Andorian dialects, so canon allows more than one dialect [ENT "Vox Sola", 2151].

During the Coalition of Planets conference six universal translators failed and confused Andorian with Rigelian [ENT "Terra Prime", 2155].

When the USS Discovery's translator malfunctioned, Lieutenant Detmer spoke a line captioned as Andorian: "Endon deth pednog. Arabic kayenn?", meaning "I can't read my console. Is this Arabic?" [DIS "An Obol for Charon", 2257]. This is the only full sentence of Andorian on screen.

Sybok said the Andorian name for the place where creation began is unpronounceable [Star Trek V].

Soval told Shran that his name would one day be the Andorian word for a fool, as Nirak became in Vulcan [ENT "Kir'Shara", 2154].

## Written Andorian

An Andorian script appears on banners at the Interspecies Medical Exchange [ENT "Stigma"] and in the Code of the Ushaan shown on screen. It is drawn in curved horizontal strokes and circles.

## Words and names attested on screen

The ushaan is an Andorian code of honour: a duel to the death, fought with an ushaan-tor, an ice miner's tool with a curved blade [ENT "United"].

Weytahn is the Andorian name of a class D planetoid on the frontier with Vulcan, which the Vulcans annexed in 2097 and renamed Paan Mokar [ENT "Cease Fire"].

Andoria is the Andorians' icy moon homeworld, orbiting a gas giant; Andor is also used on screen.

The Aenar are a pale, blind, telepathic subspecies of Andorians who live in underground cities beneath the Northern Wastes of Andoria. Andorians thought them a myth until about 2104. They are strict pacifists and numbered only a few thousand in the 2150s [ENT "The Aenar"].

Thy'lek is Shran's given name; Thy'lek Shran commanded Imperial Guard forces in the 2150s [ENT].

Kumari is the name of Shran's Imperial Guard battle cruiser [ENT "Proving Ground", "United"].

Other Andorian names on screen: Shran; Talas (Shran's companion); Tarah (Shran's first officer) and Telev (an Imperial Guard captain) [ENT "Cease Fire"]; Tholos [ENT "The Andorian Incident"]; Shras (an ambassador) [TOS "Journey to Babel"].

Aenar names on screen: Jhamel and her brother Gareb, and Lissan, who speaks for the Aenar council [ENT "The Aenar"]; Talla, the daughter of Jhamel and Shran [ENT "These Are the Voyages..."]; Hemmer, the Aenar chief engineer of the USS Enterprise in the 2250s [SNW].

## Body and senses on screen

Andorians have blue skin and two antennae, which move with emotion and are used for balance and sensing; losing one is a serious injury that takes time to regrow [ENT "United"]. Aenar are blind and telepathic, and Hemmer considered his telepathic senses superior to sight [SNW "Strange New Worlds"].

## Society on screen

Andorians are known for their military: the Andorian Imperial Guard. Shran speaks of honour, debt and vengeance; insults outsiders as "pink skins". Data mentions an Andorian wedding involving a four-person marriage [TNG "Data's Day"].
