# Andorii in the licensed novels and games (licensed)

Compiled for the UTP from Memory Beta (memory-beta.fandom.com: "Andorian", "Charan", "Thirishar ch'Thane", "Shran's family") and the FASA sourcebook as reported by Memory Alpha, November 2026. Summaries in the compiler's words, with the original source in brackets.

## Names of the language

The novels call the main Andorian language Andorii [Abyss; This Gray Spirit; Paradigm]. The FASA supplement Among the Clans calls it Graalek, says Andor means "world", and says the suffix -ia raises a word to prominence over others, which explains Andor and Andoria [FASA, Among the Clans].

## Four sexes

The Deep Space Nine relaunch novels extend the four-person marriage into four sexes. In Andorii [DS9 novel: Avatar, Book Two, and later novels]:

- thaan: roughly masculine; contributes a gamete.
- chan: roughly masculine, smaller and wiry; contributes a gamete.
- shen: roughly feminine; produces the egg, where all Andorian life begins.
- zhen: roughly feminine; carries the child to term but does not contribute genetically.

With other species, Andorians accept "male" and "female" and he or she, as long as the right analogue is used.

## Name prefixes

A full formal name has a given name, then a sex prefix before the clan name: zh' for a zhen, sh' for a shen, th' for a thaan, ch' for a chan. Examples: Thirishar ch'Thane (Shar), Charivretha zh'Thane, Thirijhamel zh'Dhaven (the full name of Jhamel), Anishtalla zh'Dhaven (Talla), ThorshkukJini zh'Thresh. The short canon names are the last part of the given name: Thirishar gives Shar, Thirijhamel gives Jhamel, Anishtalla gives Talla.

## Kinship words

Each sex has its own parent word: zhavey, the zhen parent (Shar's zhavey Charivretha); shreya, the shen parent; thavan, the thaan parent; charan, the chan parent. A child is a chei or a thei [Memory Beta, "Shran's family"; DS9 relaunch novels].

## Bonding

A union of all four sexes is needed for children; the four bond together as a quad, and the fertile period is short. A reproductive crisis threatens the species in the 24th century [DS9 relaunch novels].
