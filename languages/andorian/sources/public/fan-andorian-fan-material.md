# Andorian in fan wikis and fan conlangs (fan)

Compiled for the UTP in November 2026. These are fan works: used where they fit, never forced.

- The bonding act of the four is called shelthreth, and polite forms of address by sex are Zha, Sha, Cha and Tha [LCARS fan wiki, "Andorian Gender"].
- Andorians are said to sense vibration and pitch acutely through their antennae, and music is said to be the original basis of the Andorian language; the flabbjellah is named as both a sidearm and a musical instrument [Memory Delta fan wiki, "Andorian"].
- An'dorian is a fan conlang by Spence Hill (1990) with over 3000 words, eight noun classes and an elaborate tense, mood and aspect system [Langmaker; FrathWiki].
- Aenar is pronounced "EEH-nar" [Star Trek Expanded Universe fan wiki].
